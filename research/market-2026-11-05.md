## Method
- Pulled the **entire Coinbase CDP Bazaar discovery index**: `GET https://api.cdp.coinbase.com/platform/v2/x402/discovery/resources?limit=1000&offset=N` → **115,010 resources** (x402Version 2). Each item carries `quality.l30DaysTotalCalls`, `quality.l30DaysUniquePayers`, `lastCalledAt` — real facilitator-settled usage, last 30 days.
- Ranked by **unique payers** (harder to wash than calls) and cross-checked calls×price.
- Caveat: many hosts show payers≈calls (each payer calls once) → explorer/crawler agents that pay $0.001–0.02 to try every listed endpoint. Others show calls≫payers (repeat customers, or wash: market.datapackvibe.com 401k calls from 3 payers across 78,785 resources).

## Gaps → decisions
1. **On-chain is the #1 paid category by payers** and is near-zero cost (public RPC). Competitors are one-fact-per-call and Ethereum-only → ship **/wallet** (native + 7 majors, 5 chains, one call, $0.003) and **/tx** (status/confirmations/fee/decoded USDC transfers, $0.003) — agents paying x402 need "did my payment land?".
2. **Token verdicts sell at $0.02 with 58k calls** (ax1) but are LLM opinion → ship **/token-check** $0.01: deterministic owner/proxy/supply + DEX + on-chain Uniswap-v3 liquidity + flags/score.
3. **FX/price is #4** with 14k payers; ECB rates are free/official → **/fx** $0.002.
4. Breadth matters: explorer agents pay once per listed resource (onesource gets 100–500 payers per endpoint). More distinct, honest resources in Bazaar = bigger web.
5. Not pursued (cost/legal): LLM inference resale, Exa/search resale (paid upstream), Twitter data (ToS).
